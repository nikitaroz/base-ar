The tap is ignored (`PlacementARView.swift:815-816`), `syncGuide`, `confirmBatterySpot`, and `flipBatterySide` have no callers (`:1972-2001`), and the tone is hard-coded `.incomplete` (`:184`). The fix is in [status P0-1](repo-status-2026-09-26.md#p0-stops-a-valid-survey-or-breaks-the-evidence-rules). It keeps tap-to-place off, which differs from `AGENTS.md` (contradiction 13).
2. **Keep the scene snapshot fresh.** `emitPlanesIfNeeded` returns early when there is no battery rig (`PlacementARView.swift:1040-1041`). Submit therefore commits a stale snapshot, and transfer-switch space is scored on old mesh. Emit when any lock exists ([design §7 task 1](scan-coverage-design.md#7-implementation-tasks-in-order-sized-for-a-cursor-agent)). Two related bugs: the transfer result is sticky (`PlacementMeasuring.swift:167-169`, P1-1), and "Start over" does not clear `session.placement` (P1-2, not device-tested).
3. **Stop false reds.** The same-wall check fails "opposite sides of the same wall", a layout Base allows, and one required conflict turns the whole preview red (`PlacementMeasuring.swift:492-501`, `Rules/EligibilityRule.swift:41-43`; [guide §2 ③](electrical-equipment-guide.md#2-each-item)). The meter-height rule also enforces a 3 ft minimum that Base does not publish (`BaseRuleSet.swift:14`).
4. **Fix the evidence labels on `main` first (P0-2, P0-3, P0-4).** "No gas meter" counts as a measured pass. The 200 A panel rule decides from the main breaker. An OCR read fills the meter number with no confirm step. These fixes are small, and `BaseRuleSet.swift` merges without conflicts ([status, consolidation order](repo-status-2026-09-26.md#recommended-consolidation-order-recommendation-only)). P0-2 needs a team decision first (contradiction 10).
5. **Normalize mesh face normals.** `main` stores the raw cross product (`PlacementARView.swift:2506-2511`). As a result, `isOnWall`'s `|normal.y| < 0.45` test (`PlacementMeasuring.swift:645`) passes for nearly every face, and `unit()` drops faces smaller than about 5 cm². Ground coverage also counts a face at any height, so an eave over the pad reads as "ground seen" (`:748`). Both skew today's transfer, window, and obstacle results ([design §3.3](scan-coverage-design.md#33-cell-occupancy-from-the-mesh), [§6](scan-coverage-design.md#6-current-code-vs-this-design)).
6. **Build the coverage grades** ([design §7 tasks 2–7, 11–14](scan-coverage-design.md#7-implementation-tasks-in-order-sized-for-a-cursor-agent)). Score a 3 × 3 × 2.5 m box at the meter wall in 10 cm cells. A view counts only with `.normal` tracking, 0.3–4 m range, at most 60° off the surface normal, and medium or high depth confidence that agrees with the mesh within 5 cm. The grades are `missing`, `uncertain`, `locallyUsable`, and `unavailable`. Mesh checks pass only on `locallyUsable`, and distances get guard bands. Coverage does not change what placement color means ([design §1](scan-coverage-design.md#1-tldr)).
7. **Show one coaching line and one neutral tint** in place of the look-around checklist on LiDAR phones, and show grades on Review ([design §4](scan-coverage-design.md#4-coaching-copy), tasks 8–10 and 15). Never lock Submit behind coverage. Then spend an afternoon outdoors with a device. Tune [§3.8](scan-coverage-design.md#38-settings-to-tune-on-device), tape the wall clearance at about 1 m and the meter distance at about 6 m (task 16), and check the −yaw/+yaw working-space slab mismatch ([guide §2 ①](electrical-equipment-guide.md#2-each-item)).
8. **Close the photo gap.** The app saves 1 of Base's 9 photo compositions (`meter.jpg`). It has no breaker, disconnect, adjacent-wall, behind-the-fence, or area-around photos ([guide §3](electrical-equipment-guide.md#3-bases-nine-photos-vs-the-app), P1-6). ## Also worth knowing

These items are in `sources/` but none of the three docs covers them.

- **Handling the phone outdoors.** This is vendor advice and was not tested with this app. Move your body rather than rotating the phone, and keep a feature in frame: the meter, a corner, or a window edge. SiteScape says a phone above about 75°F throttles, which is likely on an Austin afternoon ([research, "One imperative"](sources/scan-completeness-research.md)).
- **A gap near 5 cm may be the sensor's floor, not a real opening.** The detection limit reported by Luetzenburg 2021 is about 5 cm, so do not read a one-cell hole as a clearance gap.
- **Walking the path again helps.** Treccani 2024 found that apps which closed the trajectory loop did better. Re-observing the path reduces drift, so a "walk back to the meter" step could tighten the 20 ft figure. Not tested with ARKit.
- **Tape the few tight dimensions.** Twindo tells users to tape the dimensions that must be exact. The app's tape mode exists, but this screen always passes `measurementMode: false` (`PlacementARView.swift:175`).
- **The AR screen never checks camera permission.** If access is denied, the only sign is the `session(_:didFailWithError:)` text in the status caption (`PlacementARView.swift:1007-1012`, `:190`, `:217`). The Electrical screen does check (`ElectricalCaptureView.swift:87`). From reading only.
- **Units.** Meters are used internally and converted to feet with `/0.3048` (`BatteryGeometry.swift:11`, `PlacementMeasuring.swift:482`, `:489`).
